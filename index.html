About the Studio

Quophi Keyz Music Studio is a music production space based in Ablekuma Fanmilk, Accra, Ghana. We work with upcoming artists, independent musicians, gospel artists, singers and songwriters who want to develop their sound and bring their musical ideas to life. From recording to production, mixing and mastering, our goal is to support artists at different stages of their musical journey.

  • Creativity
  • Artist development
  • Accessible services
  • Music quality
  • Support for independent artists
  • Showcasing original music
